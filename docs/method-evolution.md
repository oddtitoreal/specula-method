# Method Evolution Log
## Progressive definition of the Specula Method

This file tracks the evolution of the Specula Method and defines, step by step, what the method is at each version. It is not a technical changelog; it is a progressive definition.

---

## How to use this log
- Each version adds, clarifies, or refines what the method *is*.
- When updating the method, record: what changed, why, and what it enables or excludes.
- Keep the language operational, not marketing.

---

## Versions

### v1.0.1 (February 2026)
**Definition**: Patch update of the open documentation profile, keeping the six-phase public structure and improving runtime alignment clarity.

**Core updates**:
- Runtime-compatible schema profile explicitly documented and linked.
- Agent protocol constraints hardened (`MODE | PHASE`, canonical phase sequence with `0` and `1.5`).
- Mapping intent clarified against the private v2.4.5.x evolution track without changing the public six-phase model.

**What this enables**:
- More consistent agent outputs across workshop and runtime contexts.
- Lower integration ambiguity between open method assets and executable framework runtimes.

**What this does not change**:
- Public method release remains `v1.x`.
- Six-phase public narrative and ethical structure remain unchanged.

---

### v1.0 (January 2026)
**Definition**: A six-phase strategic foresight branding method that uses scenarios to build living brand identities oriented to preferable futures, with a formal ethical gate and a long-term governance layer.

**Core structure**:
- Six phases: Scenario Generation, Competitive Futures Mapping, Brand Archeology, Future Prototyping & Ethical Gate, Narrative Synthesis, Activation & Specula Guardian.
- Ethical Gate as non-negotiable checkpoint with documented refusals.
- Specula Guardian as ongoing monitoring and re-speculation trigger.

**What this enables**:
- Cross-scenario coherence without collapsing to a single "best" future.
- Explicit refusal policy as part of identity.
- Continuous adaptation anchored to radical values.

**What this excludes**:
- Single-scenario, campaign-only branding.
- Purpose statements without operational commitments.

### pre-1.0 (Foundational draft)
**Definition**: Early specification of Specula as a speculative identity method merging futures thinking, brand strategy, and AI-assisted divergence.

**Core structure**:
- Multi-scenario exploration as the starting point.
- Brand identity anchored to non-negotiable values.
- AI as a divergence partner, not as a shortcut.

**Limitations**:
- Governance and monitoring layers not fully formalized.
- Templates and refusal registry still embryonic.

### v1.1 (June 2026)
**Definition**: Expanded method integrating three new operational "bridges" from the v2.4.5.2 private evolution track: Confrontation & Discursive Prototyping, a strengthened Ethical + Impact Gate, and Platforming as a continuous organizational capability.

**Core updates**:
- **Phase reordering**: Framing & Ethical DNA (formerly Brand Archeology) moved to Phase 1, before Scanning. Ethics-first is now structural, not sequential.
- **New Phase 4 — Confrontation & Discursive Prototyping**: Makes scenarios "live" through discursive artifacts, role-play, and situated exhibits. Community co-creation explicitly scoped to implementation of values, not values themselves.
- **Ethical Gate → Ethical + Impact Gate (Phase 5)**: Same three core questions, now enriched with a systematic bias/impact assessment layer covering social, environmental, legal, and reputational risks; robustness across scenarios; and 30–90 day feasibility.
- **New Phase 7 — Platforming**: Converts speculative work from episodic project to continuous organizational capability, with governance, rituals, repository, and integrated AI roles.
- **Narrative Synthesis**: Demoted from standalone phase to sub-phase 6.1 of Action Design, clarifying its role as synthesis rather than endpoint.
- **Daily-life scenario format**: First-person vignettes, present tense, with explicit indicators.
- **Explicit roles**: Brand Alchemist, Futures Scout, Narrative Architect, Facilitator, Platform Steward (human); Value Guardian, Signal Harvester, Scenario Editor, Risk & Bias Spotter (AI).
- **Anti-fuffa metrics**: Learning Velocity, Decision Lead Time, Scenario Adoption, Brand Coherence Index, Ethical Incident Rate.
- **4–6 week rapid implementation checklist** added.
- **Refusal Register** formalized as a structured artifact (JSON schema in specula-framework).

**What this enables**:
- The method now covers the full arc: from initial framing to continuous platform governance.
- Scenarios become operational tools, not conceptual deliverables — they are tested against real people and real contexts before decisions are made.
- Impact assessment is integrated into the ethical gate, not run separately.

**What this does not change**:
- Core philosophy: futures as tools, not predictions.
- Radical values as invariant foundation.
- AI as divergence partner, not oracle or decision-maker.
- Mandatory human validation for all ethical decisions.
- Public method remains v1.x; private framework track remains v2.x.

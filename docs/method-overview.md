# Specula Method Overview
## Strategic Foresight Branding, v2.0

This document provides a high-level overview of the complete Specula Method. For detailed operational guidance, see the templates directory and the workshop protocols in [`specula-bos`](https://github.com/oddtitoreal/specula-bos/tree/main/workshops).

## The Core Question

Traditional branding asks: "How do we position ourselves in today's market?"

Specula asks: "What futures do we want to help make possible?"

From that question, we work backward to design identities, products, services, and AI agents that can operate in those futures without losing deep coherence.

---

## The Seven Non-Negotiable Principles

1. **Plurality before synthesis**: There is no single future.
2. **Preferable ≠ comfortable**: The preferable is political. It includes conflicts and trade-offs.
3. **Everyday futures**: Scenarios must touch rituals, micro-decisions, real life.
4. **Evidence + imagination**: Signals and data feed the process; imagination builds the scenarios.
5. **Ethics as architecture**: A structure that guides choices throughout the process, from the first question to the last review.
6. **Artifacts that generate conversations**: Prototypes and narratives are tools for debate.
7. **From workshop to platform**: A living brand requires continuity, governance, and rituals.

---

## The Process: Six Phases, One Canonical Sequence

The sequence below is the one used by the Brand Operating System ([`specula-bos`](https://github.com/oddtitoreal/specula-bos)), by the runtime schemas in [`specula-framework`](https://github.com/oddtitoreal/specula-framework) (`phase0` to `phase6`) and by the Specula Handbook. It follows the arc of any futures exercise: framing, documentation, exploration, action.

The order carries the method's central claim. Scenarios come before values because the scenarios are the test that produces the values: the Speculative Brand DNA is what remains standing after the brand's candidate values have been projected into divergent worlds.

| Phase | BOS layer | Workshop | Main output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 · Threshold & Framing | precedes the BOS | Brief Under Stress | Threshold sheet, modulated brief, written consensus |
| 1 · Scenario Generation | 01 WORLD, 02 FUTURES | Weak Signals Map, Collaborative Worldbuilding | Signals library, four divergent scenarios |
| 1.5 · Competitive Futures Mapping | refines 02 FUTURES | inside Worldbuilding | White spaces, labelled trend or countertrend |
| 2 · Brand Archaeology & Value Stress Test | 03 IDENTITY | Brand Archaeology | Speculative Brand DNA, red lines |
| 3 · Future Prototyping & Ethical + Impact Gate | 04 EXPLORATION | Confrontation: the Brand in the Futures | Validated prototypes, Refusal Register |
| 4 · Narrative Synthesis & Strategic Protocol | 05 STRATEGY | From Prototype to Protocol | Preferable future, backcasting, criteria as hypotheses, Decision Log |
| 5 · Co-creation & Community Governance | 06 GOVERNANCE | Radar Calibration, Behavioral Matrix | Brand Radar, behavioral matrix, governance playbook |
| 6 · Activation & Specula Guardian | 07 INTERFACE | Agent Onboarding | Activation roadmap, Guardian Loop |

### Phase 0: Threshold & Framing
**Duration**: 1–2 weeks (preliminary meetings plus the 90-minute Brief Under Stress)
**Output**: Threshold Sheet, Modulated Brief, Written Consensus

Building the container and the question before generating content.

**Threshold conditions, verified with observable evidence**:
- **Real mandate**: the person who signs can decide on brand and budget, and takes part in the process.
- **Recognized crisis**: the organization names the crisis in an internal document. Almost every organization lives inside some structural crisis; what counts is that this one recognizes its own.
- **Willingness to subtract**: the client lists in advance what it is ready to remove.

A Threshold Sheet records the evidence for each condition. It is archived for every engagement, including those that do not start.

**Key activities**:
- Put the existing brief under stress: implicit assumptions are surfaced, named as tensions, and reframed as speculative How Might We questions (see [The Brief Under Stress](https://github.com/oddtitoreal/specula-bos/blob/main/workshops/brief-sotto-stress.md))
- Opening exercise: how different was the organization ten years ago, and how different could it be ten years from now
- Agree in writing on three things: the research question, the time window, and the working rules
- Working rules protect free speech when the person who decides sits in the group: written contributions collected before discussion, and the decision-maker speaks last
- Set up AI: policy, datasets, access, privacy (GDPR / DPIA if required)

**Deliverables**: Threshold Sheet · Modulated Brief · Written Consensus (question, window, rules) · Stakeholder Map · AI & Data Checklist

---

### Phase 1: Scenario Generation
**Duration**: 2–3 weeks
**Output**: Signals Library, Driver & Tension Map, Four Scenario Cards, Early Warning Dashboard

**1a · Scanning & Sensemaking**

Signals are collected by people. Every participant brings five weak signals from their own domain, and at least one third come from domains outside the brand's sector. Signals are organized on a declared grid, the strategic hexagon, with the research question from Phase 0 at its centre. AI supports clustering and correlation across domains; collection stays human, because models tend to surface the signals that are already frequent.

**1b · Scenario Weaving: the construction rule**

Divergence is built into the scenarios by construction:
- **Four scenarios, one per generic future**: growth, collapse, discipline, transformation (Dator, *Alternative Futures at the Manoa School*, Journal of Futures Studies 14(2), 2009).
- **Closed megatrends appear in every scenario**. Scenarios diverge only on the open variables.
- **One scenario is built against the brand's strongest declared value**: a world where that value is costly, co-opted, or irrelevant.
- **At least one scenario should look implausible to the client at first reading.** A scenario that looks reasonable to everyone is usually an extension of the present.
- **Horizon of 10–15 years** (for example 2036–2040). Values need a long window to be tested; decisions drawn from the scenarios are later translated into 1–3 year actions.

**Each scenario includes**: a headline, a one-page daily-life vignette in present tense, key assumptions, opportunities and risks for the brand, and materialization indicators (what to observe today as confirmation or disconfirmation).

**Deliverables**: Signals Library · Driver & Tension Map · Four Scenario Cards · Early Warning Dashboard (minimum 10 signals)

---

### Phase 1.5: Competitive Futures Mapping
**Duration**: inside the Worldbuilding workshop
**Output**: Competitive Futures Map, White Spaces

Mapping competitors' speculative trajectories: research investments, partnerships, emerging languages, narrative territories. The goal is to find futures that no one is credibly occupying yet. Each white space is labelled as **trend** (part of a dominant movement) or **countertrend** (a niche that should not be mistaken for the dominant movement).

---

### Phase 2: Brand Archaeology & Value Stress Test
**Duration**: 1–2 weeks (corpus preparation, 3-hour workshop, 1 hour of AI analysis)
**Output**: Speculative Brand DNA, Red Lines, Known Tensions

Excavating what holds across divergent futures.

**The corpus of traces**:
- Decisions reconstructed from documents (minutes, budgets, offers declined), alongside the stories people tell about them
- Individual strategic interviews with different roles, external stakeholders included
- An inventory of the organization's official futures: what it has publicly said about where it is going
- Counterexamples: decisions that betrayed a declared value
- A measure of change: how much the organization has changed over the last ten years, besides what shaped it

**The Value Stress Test**:
- Each candidate value is first written in operational form ("if it does not improve X, it is not a valid choice"). The test runs on that form.
- The value is projected into each scenario. It holds if, in every scenario, it still distinguishes between options when doing so is costly.
- People who did not build the scenarios judge whether the value holds: an external reviewer, or a second team.
- Causal Layered Analysis works as a second method, reading each value at the level of worldview and metaphor.
- At least one candidate value must fall. If every value holds, the test has stressed nothing.

The Speculative Identity Model canvas (see templates) records the result: radical values, each supported by at least three documented decisions; fragile values; contingent values; red lines; tensions that remain open.

**Deliverables**: Speculative Brand DNA · Red Lines & Non-Negotiables · Known Tensions · Counterexample Log

---

### Phase 3: Future Prototyping & Ethical + Impact Gate
**Duration**: 2–3 weeks
**Output**: Discursive Prototypes Kit, Assessment Report, Refusal Register

**Confrontation & discursive prototyping**: making futures collide with people in the right context, through discursive artifacts (props, mock services, UI fiction), role-play and embodied futures, exhibits and guided sessions, "Specula Board" simulations with internal teams. The Resilience Protocol tests each prototype against four stress conditions (see templates).

**The Ethical + Impact Gate** evaluates every direction carried forward:
- Unintended impacts (social, environmental, reputational)
- Exclusions, accessibility, inequalities
- Contradictions between DNA and scenario
- Robustness: what holds across scenarios
- Feasibility: what can be tested within 30–90 days

**The three core questions**:
1. Does this violate one or more declared radical values?
2. Does it introduce manipulative, extractive, or exclusionary practices?
3. Does it create unacceptable technological, social, or environmental dependencies?

**Decision portfolio**: **GO** (we run the experiment) · **HOLD** (evidence is missing) · **STOP** (against the DNA or unacceptable risk).

**Refusal Register**: every STOP is documented with the item refused, the reasoning, the value invoked, and the risk avoided. What gets rejected becomes part of the brand's identity.

**Deliverables**: Confrontation Plan · Discursive Prototypes Kit · Assessment Report · Refusal Register · Assumption Test List (max 12)

---

### Phase 4: Narrative Synthesis & Strategic Protocol
**Duration**: 2 weeks
**Output**: Preferable Future, Backcasting Map, Narrative Playbook, Strategic Protocol, Decision Log

**The preferable future** is designed after the exploration, as a project of its own. The scenarios describe what could happen; the preferable future states what the brand will work toward, given what it has learned in them. Backcasting connects the two: for each scenario, which steps from today toward the preferable future still hold in that world.

**Narrative synthesis** turns scenarios, DNA and preferable future into an operational narrative: posture, language, priorities, and limits.

**The Strategic Protocol** translates the DNA into decision criteria. Values stay stable; the criteria that translate them into decisions are hypotheses. Each criterion carries an **expected effect** and a **review date**. New criteria are tried through experiments that can fail without damage.

**The Decision Log** opens here. Every relevant decision is recorded with the criterion applied and the expected effect, so that the Guardian can later compare it with the observed effect.

**The restitution to leadership** is designed as a design moment: a synthetic, tangible presentation of the choices, built to be decided on.

**Deliverables**: Preferable Future & Backcasting Map · Narrative Playbook · Strategic Protocol · Decision Log · Experiment Roadmap (30 / 60 / 90 days)

---

### Phase 5: Co-creation & Community Governance
**Duration**: 2–4 weeks
**Output**: Brand Radar, Behavioral Matrix, Brand Governance Playbook, Co-creation Record

**Who sets the values, and why**: the Synthesis Circle fixes the values; the community shapes how they are put into practice. The method declares who stays outside that decision and what counterweight exists: at least one member of the Circle represents the people the brand serves, and dissent is logged in the co-creation record (see the [Community Co-Creation Protocol](./community-co-creation-protocol.md)).

**Brand Radar**: five axes derived from the radical values, each with an indicator, a data source and an owner. Alert thresholds lean toward reopening, because a false alarm costs less than a late reaction. Alerts also fire on combinations of indicators, beyond single thresholds. Every escalation names people and times, scaled to the speed of the signals it watches.

**Behavioral Matrix**: decisions placed as Allowed, Sensitive, or Forbidden, built from real cases.

**Deliverables**: Brand Radar · Behavioral Matrix · Brand Governance Playbook · Co-creation Record

---

### Phase 6: Activation & Specula Guardian
**Duration**: setup in 2–4 weeks, then continuous
**Output**: Activation Roadmap, Specula Agent, Ritual Calendar, Guardian Report

**Activation and platforming**: an activation roadmap; the Specula Agent as operational interface; rituals (monthly scanning, quarterly sensemaking, semi-annual review); a repository of signals, scenarios, decisions and experiments.

**The Guardian Loop** monitors indicators and signals, updates scenarios, and compares expected and observed effects in the Decision Log. Every drift receives a classification, with the rule that produced it:

| Class | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Error** | The drift violates a red line | Correct |
| **Model superseded** | The observed world falls outside all scenarios | Re-Speculation |
| **Learning** | The drift is repeated, spontaneous and widespread, and violates no red line | The Circle deliberates whether the criterion changes |

**Emergency Re-Speculation Clause**: if signals diverge significantly from the imagined futures for two consecutive quarters, a 4-week accelerated re-speculation cycle starts.

**Definition of Done**: the engagement is complete when the client governs its BOS autonomously. The Guardian is a periodic external review that presupposes that autonomy.

**Guardian Report structure**: Period covered · Context changes · Key signals (new / accelerating / fading) · Drift classification (with evidence and rule) · Expected vs observed effects · Suggested decisions (GO / HOLD / STOP) · Required updates · Backlog (micro-actions with owner, deadline, metric)

## Time and Compression

A Full Journey takes 2–3 months and about 25 hours of workshops. A complete futures exercise in a large organization can take up to six months. Specula compresses three things:

- The preliminary meetings, into one Brief Under Stress session plus the Threshold Sheet
- Signal collection, into structured pre-work and one 3-hour session
- Scenario construction, into one 4-hour workshop on tracks prepared by the facilitator

The cost is less documentary depth and scenarios with less internal detail. The construction rule and the external judge of the Value Stress Test exist partly to offset that cost. Pilot engagements record the time spent per phase, so that the compression can be measured.

## Roles

### Human Roles
- **Brand Alchemist**: guides identity synthesis and trade-off decisions, following written synthesis rules
- **External Reviewer**: judges the Value Stress Test without having built the scenarios
- **Futures Scout**: signals, fieldwork, pattern recognition
- **Narrative Architect**: daily-life scenarios, stories, discursive artifacts
- **Facilitator**: workshops, confrontation sessions, conflict management
- **Platform Steward**: governance, rituals, repository, continuity
- **Domain Experts**: legal, product, operations, customer care, etc.

### AI Roles (optional, configurable)
- **Value Guardian**: checks coherence with the DNA
- **Signal Correlator**: proposes correlations and clusters across domains
- **Scenario Editor**: improves clarity and internal consistency of scenarios
- **Risk & Bias Spotter**: flags blind spots and unintended impacts

## Metrics

Each metric has a calculation procedure, so that two people computing it reach the same number.

- **Brand Coherence Index**: every quarter, a sample of decisions is drawn from the Decision Log. Two independent raters place each decision on the Behavioral Matrix. The index is the share of decisions both raters place as Allowed. Agreement between raters is reported with the index: when agreement is low, the matrix needs work before the index means anything.
- **Learning Velocity**: criteria reviewed per quarter, with expected and observed effects compared in the Decision Log.
- **Decision Lead Time**: time from insight to decision, read from the Decision Log.
- **Scenario Adoption**: number of functions that cite a scenario when recording a decision.
- **Ethical Incident Rate**: ethical issues flagged, and corrected, within the cycle.

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Values produced by a test
The Speculative Brand DNA is the output of a stress test against divergent futures, built with an explicit construction rule and judged by people outside the group that built the scenarios.

### 2. Structural Ethics
The Ethical + Impact Gate is a formal phase with documentation requirements. Refusal is logged and becomes identity.

### 3. Criteria that learn
Decision criteria carry an expected effect and a review date. The Guardian distinguishes errors from learning, so that coherence and adaptation can coexist.

### 4. Futures That Live
Discursive prototyping makes scenarios collide with real people in real contexts.

### 5. Platform, Not Project
The Guardian Loop turns speculative work into a continuous organizational capability.

## Phase Mapping (v1.1 → v2.0)

Version 1.1 defined the Ethical DNA before the scenarios. In that order, the Value Stress Test checks values decided in advance, and the scenarios can only confirm them. Version 2.0 restores the sequence used by `specula-bos` and by the runtime schemas: scenarios first, then the test that produces the DNA.

| v1.1 Phase | v2.0 Phase |
|---|---|
| 0. Activation & Governance | 0. Threshold & Framing |
| 1. Framing & Ethical DNA | Framing → 0. Threshold & Framing · Ethical DNA → 2. Brand Archaeology & Value Stress Test |
| 2. Scanning & Sensemaking | 1. Scenario Generation (1a) |
| 2.1 Competitive Futures Map | 1.5 Competitive Futures Mapping |
| 3. Scenario Weaving | 1. Scenario Generation (1b) |
| 4. Confrontation & Discursive Prototyping | 3. Future Prototyping & Ethical + Impact Gate |
| 5. Ethical + Impact Gate | 3. Future Prototyping & Ethical + Impact Gate |
| 6. Action Design · 6.1 Narrative Synthesis | 4. Narrative Synthesis & Strategic Protocol |
| 7. Platforming & Continuous Loop | 5. Co-creation & Community Governance (governance) · 6. Activation & Specula Guardian (rituals, repository) |
| +1 Guardian Loop | 6. Activation & Specula Guardian |
